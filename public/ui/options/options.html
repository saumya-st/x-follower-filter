X Follower Filter · Options

Labels & keywords

Each label matches when any keyword appears in an account's bio, name or handle (case-insensitive, whole words). The hint is only used by the AI labeller. Separate keywords with commas.

Pacing & safety

Actions run in a hidden background tab that clicks through X's own menus, one account at a time. Keep the pauses generous; X rate-limits aggressive accounts.

Unfollow alerts

Every few hours, rescan your followers in a small background window and tell you who unfollowed. Needs a browser window open and x.com logged in; a check takes a few minutes for large accounts. The window closes itself when done.

AI labelling (optional)

Bios are sent in batches to Anthropic's API using your key. The key is stored only in this browser's extension storage and only ever sent to api.anthropic.com. Nothing is sent anywhere until you press Run in the panel.

Data

Scanned lists, scan history and AI labels live in this browser only. Privacy policy.

Changes apply immediately to the panel after saving.
